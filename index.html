What PromptSmith does

PromptSmith makes writing tools for one-person businesses and tiny teams: prompt packs, plug-in templates, trackers, and short how-to guides. Each one is run on real jobs before it goes on sale — nothing is bought in bulk and relisted, nothing ships untested. You pay once, download at once, and get a plain setup sheet with it.

The opening product is the Small Business Prompt Set: 50 finished prompts for marketing, customer replies, product pages, social posts, and weekly planning, with matching templates and a tracker.

It is made for owners who do their own marketing between everything else — a shopkeeper, a freelancer, a team of two who need words that sound like them, without hiring a writer or sitting through a course.
